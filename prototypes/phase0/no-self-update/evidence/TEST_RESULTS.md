## What is proven

- The clean fixture passes while containing an embedded immutable catalog, an ordinary pinned artifact URL, policy prose in README/comments, and non-executable help text.
- Every negative fixture exits `1`, emits exactly one violation, and reports only its expected rule ID.
- `latest` and `main`, remote catalog and remote manifest each have separate fixtures even where they share a rule ID.
- The linter imports no filesystem mutation, network, or child-process capability. It only reads and reports to stdout.
- The prototype file list, byte lengths, and SHA-256 values are unchanged before and after all linter subprocesses.
- JSON reports use root basename and relative files rather than local absolute paths.

## What remains inferred or blocked

- **Inferred:** the policy can become a production packaging gate after the selected stack supplies a complete materialized source/config/dependency inventory.
- **Blocked:** root manifest/lock/SBOM integration, opaque bundle/native binary inspection, generated-resource coverage, and real allowlisted process-tree egress capture.
- **Not implemented:** any updater, remote catalog, update channel, telemetry, TUF/equivalent metadata, runtime downloader, or model/Comfy/H3 path.

The linter's own no-write/no-network boundary plus the before/after content snapshot supports the “no external state” conclusion for this PoC. It is not evidence about arbitrary future build adapters or packaged third-party binaries.
