# P0-ARC-012 — Resource-lease protocol with fake workers

- Owner: `/root/installer_plan_review`
- Objective: define and prove bounded leases for artifact, volume, runtime, GPU and project-run mutation before any scarce-resource work begins.
- Product boundary: leases coordinate installation and technical execution only; they do not generate media or submit user jobs.
- Accepted inputs: `AGENTS.md`, optimized architecture, root orchestration and risk register.
- Allowed writes: `docs/adr/ADR-009-resource-leases.md`, `prototypes/phase0/resource-leases/**`.
- Locks: none; fake workers only.
- Forbidden actions: no real download, model/GPU/Comfy/Desktop use, process killing outside owned fake workers, system-wide mutex/state mutation or user-file changes.
- Required evidence: lock identity, owner token, acquisition order, exclusive/shared mode where applicable, heartbeat/expiry policy, same-host liveness, stale recovery, cancellation, safe release and denial of unsafe lock stealing for artifact/volume/runtime/GPU/project resources.
- Acceptance: deterministic concurrency/fault tests prove serialization and stale recovery without corrupting the fake artifact or project state.
- Failure fallback: fail closed and require root/operator recovery; never guess that a live owner is stale.
- Unlocks after root acceptance: safe scheduling prerequisites for download, GPU certification and long-run checkpoint tasks.
