# Code of conduct

Relay's project spaces are for constructive technical collaboration.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus criticism on
the work, welcome relevant questions, protect private information, credit
sources accurately, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, or
deliberate disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action when conduct harms the project or its participants.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
or private security-reporting channel when confidentiality is required.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, recurrence, and the
safety of affected participants. Good-faith technical disagreement is not a
conduct violation.
